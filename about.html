About Joshua Generation Parish

We are Joshua Generation Parish, a parish of the Redeemed Christian Church of God (RCCG), committed to raising a generation unashamedly in love with God—grounded in scriptural truth, empowered by the Holy Spirit, and dedicated to transforming generations and communities through faith.

Our Four Pillars

The foundation of everything we do

Scriptural Integrity

We stand firmly on God's Word as the absolute truth. Every teaching, decision, and action flows from biblical foundation and unwavering commitment to Scripture.

Transformed Generations

Our mission is to raise believers equipped and empowered to impact their generation. We invest in discipleship that produces lasting transformation in individuals and families.

Holiness

We pursue Christ-like living through prayer, obedience, and sanctification. Holiness is not just a goal—it's our calling and the evidence of a life transformed by God's grace.

Community Impact

Faith without works is dead. We actively serve our community with compassion, creating tangible difference through outreach, mercy, and sacrificial love for those in need.

Our RCCG Heritage

Joshua Generation Parish is deeply rooted in the Redeemed Christian Church of God (RCCG), a global movement founded on the principles of holiness, evangelism, and discipleship. As part of this vibrant body, we share in the RCCG's vision of raising believers who honor God, live righteously, and make tangible kingdom impact.

Our identity flows from RCCG's core tenets:

  • Holiness unto the LORD – A consecrated life reflecting God's character
  • Scriptural Authority – The Bible as our ultimate guide
  • Spirit Empowerment – Living and serving through the Holy Spirit's power
  • Missionary Zeal – Passionately reaching the lost and discipling believers

In Joshua Generation Parish, these values come alive through vibrant worship, intense prayer, powerful preaching, and dedicated community outreach. We are more than a local church—we are a representation of RCCG's global commitment to kingdom transformation.
