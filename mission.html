Our Mission & Vision

Joshua Generation Parish exists to raise a generation unashamedly in love with God—empowered to impact their communities, transform their families, and advance God's kingdom across nations.

Our Sacred Mission

To make heaven · To take as many people with us · To plant churches globally · To have a member of RCCG in every family · Holiness as our lifestyle · To reach every nation for Christ

Six Strategic Pillars

The foundation of our gospel mandate

1

Make Heaven

Our primary goal is personal salvation—a transformed relationship with Jesus Christ that leads to eternal life.

2

Bring Others Along

We are committed to passionate evangelism, bringing as many souls as possible into God's kingdom.

3

Global Reach

Our vision extends beyond borders—placing RCCG members in families across all nations worldwide.

4

Holy Living

Holiness isn't an aspiration—it's our lifestyle. We pursue sanctification and Christ-like living daily.

5

Church Multiplication

We establish RCCG parishes strategically, ensuring accessible spiritual community within reachable distances.

6

Nations for Christ

Every nation, every culture, every heart—reached with the transformational message of Jesus Christ.

Core Commitment

Passionate Evangelism

We don't just believe—we share. Every believer is empowered to reach the lost with urgency and love.

Discipleship Pipeline

New converts are nurtured through intentional discipleship, becoming leaders who multiply the mission.

World Transformation

We envision a world where RCCG's presence testifies to God's transformational power in every community.
