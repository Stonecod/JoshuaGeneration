A Warm Welcome

We believe that every person is created in God's image and deserves a place of belonging. At Joshua Generation Parish, we are committed to raising a generation unashamedly in love with God—rooted in truth, empowered by the Holy Spirit, and dedicated to transforming communities through Christ's love.

Join us as we worship together, grow together, and serve together in the mission of making disciples for Christ.
